What's going on at GitLab?

They released their usual monthly release 19.6.0 to their package repos (https://packages.gitlab.com/gitlab/gitlab-ee/ubuntu/jammy/pool/main/g/gitlab-ee/) but did not announce it at all as of 3.5h after release (https://docs.gitlab.com/releases/all-releases.xml, https://docs.gitlab.com/releases/19/gitlab-19-6-released/).

The pre-release info on 19.6.0 says that it addresses nginx2shell: https://docs.gitlab.com/update/versions/gitlab_19_changes/#n...

There is also a workaround to disable http2 from NGINX and make that part safe. I'm leaving HTTP2 disabled for the forseeable future. - edit /etc/gitlab/gitlab.rb - add lines: gitlab_rails['nginx']['http2_enabled'] = false nginx['http2_enabled'] = false - save and run: gitlab-ctl reconfigure - finally run: gitlab-ctl restart nginx

There is no workaorund for Ruby2Shell though :( As it was just dropped last night, well after they would have started testing etc for 19.6.0, it's no surprise it's not in there. Also, since they konw 19.6.0 has Ruby2Shell in it, it's not much surprise they haven't been promoting it, or that they are scrambling.

GitLab is such a huge beast that it can't be quick to get changes made and run through tests for everything. Who knows what else the runtime update caused to fail. I imagine they are working on it.. meanwhile I firewalled the web of any of the ones I take care of. IMO having gitlab open to the public web is insanity personified.. I sure wouldn't do that with my instance!
